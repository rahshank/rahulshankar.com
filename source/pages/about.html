Hey, I'm Rahul. I grew up in Bombay and moved to the United States in my early twenties. I have spent most of my adult life here, with Detroit as the city that has made the strongest claim on me.

I run a dinner pop-up in the city called Ghosts of Bombay. The food is inspired by the memories, flavors, and food traditions of Bombay.

Outside the kitchen, I work at Slalom. I started in consulting; these days my work is closer to product. I lead platform operations and intelligence for a small product incubator inside the firm.

You can find me on Twitter or Instagram.
