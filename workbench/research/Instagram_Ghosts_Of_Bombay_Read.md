# Instagram Ghosts Of Bombay Read

## Purpose
Capture what the current public Instagram surface says about Ghosts of Bombay for site visual direction and homepage/about positioning.

## Source
[instagram.com/ghostsofbombay](https://www.instagram.com/ghostsofbombay/)

Inspected through Chrome on 2026-05-30. Instagram showed a login wall, but the public profile, grid links, visible images, and caption alt text were accessible after closing the overlay. Codex captured a temporary local inventory and contact sheet for analysis. Source images should stay out of the public site repo unless Rahul explicitly chooses site assets.

## Public Profile
Bio: "Food inspired by the memories, flavors, and food traditions of Bombay. Popping up somewhere near you in Detroit."

Visible profile state at inspection: 26 posts, 520 followers, 59 following.

## Current Read
Instagram is the reality check against the Pinterest board. Pinterest carries the larger atmospheric world. Instagram shows the live public object: pop-ups, menus, food, collaborators, neighborhood bars, charity events, and a strong recurring mark.

The strongest visual system is navy plus coral/orange, with cream/pink menu cards and warm food photography. The account already has a usable public language: specific dishes, dates, collaborators, nonprofit/community context, and occasional personal memory.

## Recurring Signals
| Signal | Site Translation |
| --- | --- |
| Navy circular logo with coral/orange type | Use navy as the main Ghosts of Bombay accent, not as the entire personal-site background |
| Cream and pale pink menu cards | Project/menu surfaces can use softer paper tones inside the broader site palette |
| Food close-ups | Use documentary images where they carry evidence of real events |
| Event flyers | A project page can have flyer-like blocks without turning the whole site into a poster |
| Collaborators and venues | About and project pages should name the public life of the project, not only the concept |
| Charity/community posts | Ghosts of Bombay reads as civic and hosted, not only culinary or nostalgic |
| Long captions with food history and memory | Notebook can hold this voice better than polished brand copy |

## Palette Adjustment
| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ghosts navy | `#202070` | Ghosts of Bombay marks, project accents, selected links |
| Coral type | `#f06f5f` | small highlights, marks, hover accents |
| Menu paper | `#f5eee5` | menu/project cards, not full-site default |
| Pale rose | `#ead0cf` | secondary Ghosts surfaces |
| Food red | `#b03040` | restrained food/event accents |
| Fresh green | `#2f9a35` | food photography accent only |

## Design Consequences
- Treat Instagram as the current public proof layer.
- Keep the personal site text-led; let Ghosts of Bombay appear through project pages, notes, selected images, and small color decisions.
- Use the logo colors sparingly so they stay distinctive.
- Preserve the caption voice: specific dishes, real venues, real collaborators, and occasional memory.
- Do not flatten the project into a restaurant brand or a generic chef portfolio.
