# Visual Direction Research

## Purpose
Track visual choices for the personal site before changing the system.

## What To Research
| Layer | Research Question | Good Sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type | Should the site read as essay archive, notebook, product-minded personal site, or culinary/public-life surface? | personal sites, small presses, food menus, city/archive publications |
| Color | What palette can hold Bombay, Detroit, writing, and product work without becoming a theme restaurant or SaaS page? | old city signage, book covers, restaurant ephemera, archive sites, print journals |
| Layout | How sparse should the homepage be, and how dense should the writing index be? | Michael Nielsen, Andy Matuschak, VGR, Matt Webb, independent blogs |
| Image treatment | Should images stay documentary, become large atmospheric anchors, or mostly appear inside posts? | migrated Ghost posts, Ghosts of Bombay photos, reference sites |
| Navigation | What top-level labels match the real site: Writing, About, Ghosts of Bombay, Notes, Projects? | current archive shape and likely next six months of use |
| Archive behavior | Should old Ghost posts look preserved, lightly normalized, or fully redesigned? | VGR/Ribbonfarm archive approach, current Ghost import |

## Palette Directions
| Direction | Feel | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper, ink, oxblood | literary, simple, already close to current site | can feel too default-blog |
| Charcoal, cream, turmeric, deep green | food memory, Bombay/Detroit warmth, restrained color | can slide into restaurant branding |
| Off-white, black, electric link color | bespoke notebook, Michael/Andy-ish utility | may underplay Ghosts of Bombay atmosphere |
| Warm gray, red oxide, brass, night blue | city, old signage, late-night hospitality | can become too designed for a personal archive |

## Type Directions
| Direction | Use Case | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Serif text with quiet sans navigation | essays first, calm reading | current version may already be too large/heavy |
| Humanist sans with serif article titles | product/practice first, still literary | can feel neither here nor there if not tuned |
| Newspaper/editorial serif system | archive, public notes, strong article voice | risks over-dramatizing short notes |
| System fonts only | maximum durability and speed | fewer signals of taste |
