# Pinterest DDC / Ghosts Of Bombay Read

## Purpose
Capture the visual direction from the private DDC / Ghosts of Bombay Pinterest board without mirroring private board images into the public repo.

## Source
Private Pinterest board: `DDC - GHOSTS OF BOMBAY`, section `Intro Flyer - INSPO`.

Inspected through Rahul's logged-in Chrome session on 2026-05-30. Codex captured a temporary local pin inventory and image contact sheet for analysis. Source images and pin dumps should stay out of the public site repo.

## Current Read
The board is strongest where it feels like a public room at night: street food, music, transit, wet pavement, poster illustration, lit buildings, and small restaurants with people spilling into the frame.

This does not point toward polished hospitality branding. It points toward a personal site with a quiet reading surface and a hotter accent layer: ink, paper, night blue, red oxide, turmeric yellow, deep green, cobalt, and occasional pink/orange.

Filter out stray pins that read as unrelated fashion/lifestyle references: clothing, jewelry, hair/fashion imagery, and the beach dog image should not drive the visual direction.

## Recurring Motifs
| Motif | Site Translation |
| --- | --- |
| Night streets and lit facades | Dark accent panels, night-blue links, restrained shadows |
| Auto rickshaws, buses, streets, roadside eating | Notebook and field-note sections can feel observational and place-based |
| Jazz / drums / small music rooms | Rhythm in headings, section breaks, and short notes; avoid overly sterile spacing |
| Poster illustration and flat color | Use flat color blocks, rules, and simple swatches before photographic decoration |
| Restaurant tables and temporary public rooms | Ghosts of Bombay pages can feel hosted without becoming restaurant-template pages |
| Red interiors, yellow light, blue/orange figures | Accent palette should be saturated but used sparingly |
| Food close-ups and spice texture | Article images can stay documentary; color system can carry the atmosphere |

## Design Consequences
- Keep the base site closer to Michael / VGR: durable, text-led, locally generated, low maintenance.
- Let Ghosts of Bombay enter through accents, image rhythm, notebook categories, and occasional project pages.
- Avoid a full-screen restaurant aesthetic for the whole personal site.
- Avoid glossy portfolio styling, pastel lifestyle styling, and generic cream-and-serif literary branding.
- Use photos and migrated images as evidence, not decoration.

## Open Questions
- Should Ghosts of Bombay get its own project page, or remain present through About, Notebook, and selected essays?
- Should the homepage have one visual accent from this world, or stay all text until a Ghosts of Bombay page exists?
- Which font pairing can hold both long essays and poster-like project energy without feeling branded?
