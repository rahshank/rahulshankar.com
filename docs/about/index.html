About

Hey, I'm Rahul. I grew up in Bombay, moved to the United States in my early 20s, and have spent the past 15 years in Detroit.

I spend a lot of time thinking about gathering, scenes, technology, creative output, and a life well lived. In late 2022 I started a dinner pop-up series called Detroit Dinner Club with a few friends.

When I'm not playing in the kitchen and thinking about the menu for the next pop-up, my day job involves working at the consulting firm Slalom. I try to study how the world, and the best-performing companies and people operate. I use what I learn to help companies design better systems, products, and ways of working.

You can find me on Twitter or Instagram.
